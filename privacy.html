Cookies and Tracking Technologies

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small data files stored on your device that help websites remember information about you. We use cookies and similar technologies (such as web beacons and pixel tags) to enhance your browsing experience, remember your preferences, and gather analytics about how our website is used.

Types of Cookies We Use

  • Essential Cookies: Required for the website to function properly and enable core functionality such as security and network management.
  • Analytical Cookies: Help us understand how visitors interact with our website by collecting and reporting information about website usage.
  • Marketing Cookies: Used to track visitors across websites and deliver personalized advertisements based on their interests and browsing history.
  • Preference Cookies: Remember your preferences and choices to enhance your experience on our website.

Managing Cookies

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. You can choose to disable cookies or receive a warning before a cookie is stored. However, disabling cookies may affect the functionality and user experience of our website. For more information about managing cookies, please visit your browser's help section or www.allaboutcookies.org.

We also respect Do Not Track (DNT) signals sent by your browser. If you enable DNT, we will make reasonable efforts to honor your preference, though some functionality may be limited.

  • • Access controls and authentication mechanisms
  • • Employee training on data protection and privacy practices

However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security. You acknowledge and accept the inherent risks associated with online communication and data storage.
